ABOUT STUDYSPOT

Making Campus Study Easier

StudySpot is a campus-based platform designed to help students find the right place to study according to their needs.

Students can explore libraries, reading rooms, classrooms and other study spaces, check their current crowd status, join study groups and share their experience with other students.
